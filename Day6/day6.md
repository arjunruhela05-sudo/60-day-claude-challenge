# Day 6 - Resume Optimization & ATS Analysis

## Objective

Today I learned how Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) evaluate resumes and how AI can be used to optimize a resume for better visibility and recruiter readability.

---

## Activity Performed

1. Uploaded my resume to Claude.
2. Used the Resume Optimizer prompt.
3. Reviewed the ATS analysis provided by Claude.
4. Identified strengths and weaknesses in my resume.
5. Generated an optimized one-page version of my resume.
6. Compared the original and optimized versions.

---

## ATS Analysis Summary

### Strengths Identified

* Clear education section
* Relevant technical skills included
* Good foundation for entry-level opportunities

### Areas for Improvement

* Better keyword optimization
* Stronger project descriptions
* More action-oriented bullet points
* Improved formatting for ATS parsing
* Enhanced readability and structure

---

## Changes Made in the Optimized Resume

### Before Optimization

* Generic descriptions
* Limited ATS keywords
* Less emphasis on achievements
* Basic formatting

### After Optimization

* Improved ATS-friendly structure
* Better keyword alignment
* Stronger project descriptions
* More professional presentation
* Enhanced readability

---

## Key Learnings

* ATS systems scan resumes for relevant keywords.
* Resume formatting affects ATS parsing.
* Quantifiable achievements improve resume quality.
* Strong action verbs make experience descriptions more impactful.
* AI can significantly improve resume quality and ATS compatibility.

---

## Biggest Insight

A resume should not only look professional to humans but also be structured so that ATS software can correctly understand and rank it.

Today I learned that resume optimization is more than formatting. ATS-friendly resumes increase the chances of passing initial screening processes and reaching recruiters. Using AI helped me identify weaknesses and create a stronger, more professional resume.
