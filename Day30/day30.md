# Day 30 – Supply Chain Optimizer Simulator

## Overview

For Day 30 of the Claude AI 60 Days Challenge, I built an interactive **Supply Chain Optimizer Simulator**. The application demonstrates how strategic supply chain decisions impact overall business performance through an engaging, scenario-based simulation.

## Features

* Random company profile generation
* Supplier strategy selection
* Factory location planning
* Warehouse strategy optimization
* Transportation method selection
* Inventory strategy management
* Real-time business metric updates
* Overall Supply Chain Score
* Strengths and weaknesses analysis
* Risk identification
* AI-generated improvement recommendations
* Replay with multiple business scenarios

## Technologies Used

* HTML5
* Tailwind CSS
* Vanilla JavaScript

## Key Learnings

* Fundamentals of supply chain optimization.
* Trade-offs between cost, speed, and operational efficiency.
* How different logistics decisions affect overall business performance.
* Building interactive educational simulations with AI assistance.
* Creating responsive single-file web applications.
