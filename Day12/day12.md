# Day 12 – AI Job Search & Personal Branding Toolkit

## Objective

Learn how AI can be used to create a complete Job Search and Personal Branding Toolkit by analyzing a resume, target role, job description, and career goals.

---

## Task Completed

### Step 1: Resume Analysis

Uploaded my latest resume and selected my target role.

The AI analyzed:

* Technical skills
* Projects
* Resume structure
* ATS readiness
* Career positioning
* Professional branding

---

### Step 2: Job Description Analysis

Provided a target Job Description (JD).

The analysis included:

* Required skills
* Preferred qualifications
* Technical requirements
* Industry keywords
* Role expectations

---

### Step 3: Personal Branding Toolkit Generation

Generated a complete career toolkit including:

* ATS Resume Recommendations
* Personal Branding Strategy
* LinkedIn Optimization Suggestions
* Skill Gap Analysis
* Career Roadmap
* Interview Preparation Guidance
* Job Search Strategy

## Key Learnings

### 1. Personal Branding Matters

Recruiters evaluate not only skills but also online presence and professional presentation.

### 2. ATS Optimization Increases Visibility

Using relevant keywords improves chances of passing ATS screening.

### 3. Skill Gaps Can Be Identified Early

AI can highlight important technologies and skills required by the market.

### 4. LinkedIn Is a Powerful Career Tool

A strong LinkedIn profile can increase recruiter visibility and networking opportunities.

### 5. Career Planning Becomes Easier With AI

AI can create structured action plans based on career goals and target roles.
