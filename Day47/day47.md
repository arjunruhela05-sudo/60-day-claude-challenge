# 🚀 Day 47/60 – Content Intelligence Studio

## 📌 Overview

For Day 47 of my Claude AI Challenge, I built **Content Intelligence Studio**, a modern AI-powered dashboard that helps creators analyze and optimize their content before publishing.

The application is designed as a premium SaaS dashboard capable of reviewing both text and image content through AI-powered analysis and providing detailed recommendations to improve engagement and performance.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Develop an intelligent content analysis platform that can:

- Analyze content quality
- Detect strengths and weaknesses
- Suggest improvements
- Generate rewritten versions
- Predict content performance
- Deliver platform-specific recommendations
