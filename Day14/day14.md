# Day 14 – Job Red Flag Detector Using Claude

## Objective

The objective of this task was to analyze a job opportunity using Claude AI and identify potential risks, positive signals, and overall suitability of the role by using a Job Red Flag Detector prompt.

---

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Reviewed Learning Resources

Read all provided resources to understand the Job Red Flag Detector workflow.

### 2. Watched Solution Video

Watched the solution video to understand the expected output and report structure.

### 3. Opened Claude

Accessed Claude AI for performing the job analysis.

### 4. Set Effort Level to Low

Configured Claude's effort level to **Low** as instructed.

### 5. Gathered Job Description (JD)

Collected a job description for analysis.

### 6. Gathered Company Information

Collected information about the company including:

* Company overview

### 8. Uploaded/Pasted Job Description

Provided the job description to Claude.

### 9. Added Company Information

Included the collected company information for deeper analysis.

### 10. Generated Risk Analysis Report

Claude generated a detailed risk assessment report.

---

## Risk Analysis Report Summary

### Overall Risk Score

**Risk Score:** 4/10 (Low to Moderate Risk)

### Positive Signals

* Clear job responsibilities.
* Competitive compensation package.
* Growth and learning opportunities.
* Established company presence.
* Transparent hiring process.

### Red Flags Identified

* Limited information about career progression.
* Overtime expectations not clearly defined.
* Performance evaluation criteria not fully specified.
* Work-life balance details missing.

### Risk Breakdown

| Category                | Risk Level |
| ----------------------- | ---------- |
| Compensation            | Low        |
| Career Growth           | Medium     |
| Management Transparency | Medium     |
| Work-Life Balance       | Medium     |
| Company Stability       | Low        |

### Final Verdict

The opportunity appears generally safe and suitable for candidates seeking growth and industry exposure. However, applicants should clarify career progression paths, performance expectations, and work-life balance during the interview process.

---

## Interview Questions Generated

1. What does success look like in this role during the first six months?
2. How is employee performance evaluated?
3. What opportunities exist for promotions and career advancement?
4. How does the company support employee learning and development?
5. What are the typical working hours for this role?
6. How is feedback provided to employees?
7. What challenges is the team currently facing?
8. How would you describe the company culture?
