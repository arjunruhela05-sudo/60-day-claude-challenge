Day 16 – Fundamental Research Deep Dive
Overview

For Day 16 of my 60 Days Claude Challenge, I explored how AI can be used to perform structured fundamental stock analysis and generate a professional research dashboard.

The project focuses on Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) and presents important financial metrics, valuation indicators, growth trends, balance sheet quality, ownership structure, peer comparison, and investment watch points in a single visual report.

Objective
Transform financial data into an easy-to-understand dashboard.
Evaluate company quality using fundamental metrics.
Learn how prompt engineering can assist investment research workflows.
Practice converting raw data into actionable insights.
