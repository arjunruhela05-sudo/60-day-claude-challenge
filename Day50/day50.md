# 🛡️ Defend Your Experience

**Day 50 of the Claude AI 60 Days Challenge**

## Overview

Defend Your Experience is an AI-powered interview preparation platform that transforms resumes, portfolios, LinkedIn profiles, project descriptions, and other professional documents into realistic interview simulations.

Unlike traditional resume review tools, this application extracts every meaningful claim from uploaded content and challenges users to defend those claims through adaptive, AI-generated interview questions.

## Features

* Intelligent claim extraction
* Adaptive AI interviewer
* Personalized follow-up questioning
* Dynamic interview flow
* Weak claim detection
* Confidence scoring
* Defense strength analytics
* Interactive dashboards
* Progress tracking
* Comprehensive Defense Report
* Drag-and-drop document upload
* Local storage and session history
* Export functionality
* Responsive modern interface
* Graceful handling of API rate limits

## Tech Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript (Vanilla)
* Anthropic Messages API
* Local Storage

## How It Works

1. Upload your resume or professional document.
2. The AI extracts important claims and achievements.
3. Start an adaptive interview session.
4. Answer progressively deeper questions.
5. Receive real-time feedback and confidence scores.
6. Review a comprehensive Defense Report highlighting strengths, weak claims, and improvement opportunities.

## Purpose

The goal is not to improve a resume—it is to prepare users to confidently explain and defend every statement they make during interviews, presentations, startup pitches, and professional discussions.
