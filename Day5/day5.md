# Day 5 - Context Engineering

## Objective

Today I learned how Context Engineering improves AI-generated outputs by providing additional information and background.

---

## Prompt A (Without Context)

A basic prompt was used without providing any personal details or background information.

### Observation

* The output was generic.
* The recommendations were broad and applicable to a wide audience.
* The roadmap lacked personalization.
* The suggestions were useful but not tailored to my specific goals.

---

## Prompt B (With Context)

The prompt included personal information:

* Current Situation: Student
* Current Skills: Web Development
* Goal: Get a Job
* Timeline: 1 Year

### Observation

* The output was highly personalized.
* The roadmap was aligned with my career goals.
* The recommendations matched my current skill level.
* The learning path was more practical and actionable.

---

## Comparison

### Prompt A

* General response
* Less personalized
* Broad recommendations
* Limited relevance to my situation

### Prompt B

* Personalized roadmap
* Goal-oriented recommendations
* Better learning sequence
* More actionable steps

---

## Biggest Insight

Providing context significantly improves AI outputs. The more relevant information given to the AI, the more useful and personalized the response becomes.

---

## Key Learnings

* Context Engineering improves response quality.
* Personalized inputs create personalized outputs.
* AI performs better when it understands the user's situation and goals.
* Adding context leads to more relevant recommendations.
