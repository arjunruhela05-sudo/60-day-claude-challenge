What I've built

🍽️
⭐ Featured Project

College Canteen Management System

A full-stack web application that digitizes the daily operations of a college canteen — replacing messy paper tokens and manual billing with a clean, fast, database-driven system. Students can browse the menu, place orders, and track their order status in real time. Canteen staff get a simple admin dashboard to manage inventory, update menu items, view today's orders, and generate daily sales reports. The system tracks stock levels and flags items running low, helping the canteen avoid waste and run-outs. Built as a solo project to solve a genuine problem I saw every day on campus.
