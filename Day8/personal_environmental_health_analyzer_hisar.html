Executive Summary: Hisar, Haryana ranks among India's most polluted cities with an AQI of 156 (Unhealthy), placing it 109th globally. Annual 2026 AQI has worsened by 47% compared to 2020 levels, driven by crop burning, industrial emissions, and dust storms. PM2.5 at 62 µg/m³ is 12× above WHO guidelines. Groundwater TDS exceeds 1,100 mg/L — well above safe limits — causing documented skin, hair, and kidney issues among residents. Compared to 10 Indian cities, Hisar ranks 8th most polluted. Northeast cities like Shillong (AQI 28) and Aizawl (AQI 35) offer dramatically cleaner environments. Immediate indoor air purification and water treatment are strongly advised.

Daily actions

Check AQI on AQI.in before stepping out
Wear N95/KN95 mask outdoors, not cloth masks
Exercise before 7am or after 6pm when AQI dips
Shower after outdoor exposure to remove PM particles
Stay hydrated — pollution dehydrates respiratory lining

Indoor air improvements

Install HEPA air purifier in bedroom (CADR ≥ 200)
Keep windows shut during 6am–10am peak pollution
Add indoor plants: snake plant, peace lily, areca palm
Use exhaust fan when cooking to remove PM from kitchen
Replace AC filters monthly during high-pollution months

Hair care (hard water)

Install shower head filter (removes calcium, fluoride)
Use sulfate-free, pH-balanced shampoo (pH 4.5–5.5)
Do apple cider vinegar rinse weekly (diluted 1:5)
Deep oil massage with coconut + castor oil 2×/week
Collect and use rainwater for final hair rinse

Skin care

Apply moisturizer within 3 minutes of showering (ceramides)
Use gentle cleanser — avoid soap bars with high pH
Niacinamide serum helps combat pollution-induced acne
Double cleanse at night to remove PM2.5 from skin
SPF 50+ sunscreen daily — pollution + UV compound damage

Water quality fixes

Install RO+UV system — reduces TDS from 1100 to <150 mg/L
Test water for fluoride annually (Hisar levels exceed WHO)
Add activated carbon post-filter for chlorine removal
Use filtered water for cooking, not just drinking

Outdoor activity guide

Avoid outdoor exercise when AQI > 100
Plan heavy workouts for evenings (AQI lowest 1–5pm)
Avoid Sector 15A area — historically worst AQI in Hisar
Winter months (Oct–Feb): restrict all outdoor activity
