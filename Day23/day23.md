# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Challenge Objective

Generate a complete Customer & MVP Blueprint for the startup idea created in Day 22 using Claude AI.

## Startup

**PlacementOS** – AI-Powered Placement Preparation Platform for Engineering Students

---

## What I Completed

* Generated a detailed Customer & MVP Blueprint
* Defined the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
* Created a Buyer Persona
* Identified the Top 10 Customer Pain Points
* Mapped the Customer Journey
* Reviewed Buying Triggers & Customer Objections
* Designed an MVP Recommendation
* Applied MoSCoW Prioritization
* Created a Pricing Hypothesis
* Identified the Top 5 Startup Risks
* Built a Founder Action Sheet
* Reviewed the 30-Day MVP Plan

---

## Key Learnings

* Building for a specific customer segment is more valuable than building for everyone.
* Customer pain points should drive product decisions.
* A simple MVP focused on solving one core problem is better than a feature-heavy first version.
* Pricing should be validated before investing heavily in development.
* Customer interviews and feedback are essential before writing production code.
