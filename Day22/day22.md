# 🚀 Startup Validation Advisor Dashboard

Day 22 of my **Claude 60 Days Challenge**

## 📌 Overview

Startup Validation Advisor is an AI-powered dashboard that helps founders evaluate and validate startup ideas through structured business analysis.

Instead of manually researching every aspect of a business idea, the application collects essential startup information and generates a professional startup validation report similar to reports created by VC firms or consulting companies.

---

## ✨ Features

- Executive Summary
- Problem Validation
- Founder-Market Fit Score
- TAM, SAM & SOM Analysis
- Competitor Analysis
- Market Gap Identification
- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Buyer Persona
- Customer Pain Points
- Buying Triggers & Objections
- Customer Journey Mapping
- Risk Assessment
- Pivot Opportunities
- Go / No-Go Recommendation
- 30-Day Startup Action Plan
- Modern Analytics Dashboard UI
- Interactive Business Metrics
- Professional Report Layout

This project demonstrates how AI can assist entrepreneurs by transforming startup ideas into structured business validation reports with actionable insights.
