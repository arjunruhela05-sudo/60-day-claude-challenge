# 🏥 Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

## Overview

The **Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator** is a single-file web application that recreates the administrative workflow involved in preparing a patient for hospital admission. Users take on the role of a **Hospital Admission Coordinator** and complete tasks that improve admission readiness while managing prior authorization, insurance verification, documentation, bed assignment, and care coordination.

> **Note:** All provider names, payer names, and clinical scenarios are **illustrative training data** for educational purposes only.

## Features

* Hospital admission setup form
* Prior Authorization workflow with approval, pending, denial, and appeal paths
* Weighted Admission Readiness Score
* Insurance verification workflow
* Bed assignment management
* Clinical documentation tracking
* Physician order verification
* Patient consent completion
* Admission timeline and milestone tracking
* Care Coordination team cards
* Utilization Review information including InterQual and Milliman references
* Risk Tracking dashboard
* CMS Observation Status educational guidance
* Governance Snapshot with healthcare benchmark estimates
* Final admission readiness evaluation

## Technologies Used

* HTML5
* Tailwind CSS (CDN)
* Vanilla JavaScript

## Learning Outcomes

This project demonstrates how healthcare administrative processes can be translated into an interactive web-based simulation. It focuses on admission readiness, utilization review, prior authorization workflows, risk management, and coordinated patient intake while strengthening frontend development skills.

## Disclaimer

This application is intended **only for educational and training purposes**. It is **not** a clinical decision-support system and should not be used for real patient care or operational hospital decisions. All providers, payers, and patient scenarios are fictional or illustrative.
